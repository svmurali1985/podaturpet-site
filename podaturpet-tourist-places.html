Regional places worth exploring

A practical mix of temples, hills, water landscapes and wider day-trip ideas. These are regional destinations, not claims that every place is inside Podaturpet.

🛕
Temple · Tamil Nadu

Tiruttani Murugan Temple

A prominent hill temple in nearby Tiruttani and a natural starting point for exploring the region around Podaturpet.

Check temple timings and current road conditions before travelling.
District tourism information →
⛰️
Hill temple · Tamil Nadu

Sholingur Lakshmi Narasimha Temple

Visit the hill-temple landscape of Sholingur, where the temple and surrounding viewpoints offer a distinctive regional pilgrimage experience.

Confirm access arrangements and visitor facilities with the temple.
Official temple website →
💧
Waterfall · Andhra Pradesh

Kailasakona Waterfall

A nature destination in the Narayanavanam area of Andhra Pradesh, suitable for visitors exploring the Puttur and Nagari side of the region.

Water flow, access and safety conditions can change with the weather.
Official tourism information →
🥾
Hills · Andhra Pradesh

Nagari Hills and Nagari Nose

A striking hill landscape near Nagari and Puttur. Experienced walkers can research the Nagari Nose route before planning a responsible trek.

Verify local permissions, heat conditions and route difficulty first.
Read the route information →
🌊
Reservoir · Tiruvallur

Poondi Reservoir

Also known as Sathyamoorthy Reservoir, Poondi is a recognised Tiruvallur district destination for visitors interested in regional landscapes.

Respect restricted areas and check current visitor access.
District tourism information →
🪔
Heritage temple · Tamil Nadu

Thiruvalangadu Vadaranyeswarar Temple

Explore a historic Shiva temple at Thiruvalangadu and include the surrounding heritage landscape in a wider regional temple visit.

Confirm current opening hours and festival arrangements.
Official temple website →
🙏
Pilgrimage · Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati and Tirumala

The Tirupati–Tirumala pilgrimage region is an option for a longer trip. Use the official Devasthanams portal for genuine visitor information.

Check official darshan, accommodation and booking requirements.
Official TTD website →
🦩
Longer district trip

Pulicat and the coastal region

Pulicat is listed by Tiruvallur District Administration as a district tourism destination and is better treated as a separate, longer excursion.

Not a close-by Podaturpet attraction; plan a separate travel day.
District tourism directory →
