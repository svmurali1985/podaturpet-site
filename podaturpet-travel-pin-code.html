Podaturpet PIN code

The postal PIN code associated with Podaturpet and nearby communities in the Pallipattu area is 631208. Tiruvallur District Administration's published e-governance directory includes several local addresses using this PIN. When placing an order or arranging delivery, confirm the complete street address, recipient details and postal information with the relevant business.

Where Podaturpet is located

Podaturpet belongs to Pallipattu Taluk in Tiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu. The town is also written as Podhaturpet, Podathurpettai or similar spelling variants in different public records. Searching more than one spelling can be useful when checking maps, administrative directories or local business details.

Distance from Tiruttani and Pallipattu

The Podhaturpet Town Panchayat profile places Tiruttani approximately 21 kilometres away and Pallipattu around 10 kilometres away. These are general published reference distances; your actual driving distance and travel time will depend on the route, starting point, road conditions and current traffic.

Rail access

Tiruttani is a practical nearby town to check when planning a rail-and-road journey toward Podaturpet. Travellers should verify station availability, current train schedules and onward local transport independently because services and journey times can change.

Road and bus connection

Podaturpet can be approached by road through the wider Tiruttani and Pallipattu region. Bus services, local transport options and departure times can change, so check the current route with an operator, bus station or local contact before starting a journey.

Nearby places

Tiruttani and Pallipattu provide useful points of orientation for Podaturpet and the surrounding textile communities. The region sits in northern Tamil Nadu, near travel corridors that also connect with the Andhra Pradesh side of the wider area.

Tiruttani Murugan temple

Tiruvallur District Administration identifies Arulmigu Subramaniya Swami Temple in Tiruttani as an important religious destination. The temple is associated with Lord Murugan and is recognised among his six traditional pilgrimage abodes. Visitors travelling to Podaturpet may wish to consider Tiruttani as a nearby cultural stop, while checking opening arrangements and travel conditions separately.

Practical tips for textile buyers visiting Podaturpet

  • Confirm the exact business address and use PIN code 631208 when appropriate.
  • Arrange an appointment before visiting a supplier, trader or local contact.
  • Carry product photographs, required quantities and colour or fabric specifications.
  • Check sample availability, payment terms and delivery arrangements directly with the business.
  • Verify current road, bus and train timings before finalising your journey.
