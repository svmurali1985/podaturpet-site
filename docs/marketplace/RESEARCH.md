# Global services research and opportunity model

Review date: 19 September 2026. Forty services were evaluated. No search volumes, keyword rankings, demand counts, conversion rates or revenue forecasts were invented. Occupational and regulatory sources are contextual evidence; they are not measurements of Tamil-speaking consumers' willingness to buy in a particular city.

## Decision logic

Service Opportunity Score = weighted ratings (1–5) minus risk penalty (0–20).
Weights: recurring need 20%, problem severity 20%, language gap 15%, commercial intent 15%, source quality 10%, verifiable supply 10%, delivery feasibility 10%. Score = Σ(weight × rating / 5) − risk. All ratings and language/intent estimates are editorial inference, explicitly stored with each record in content/services.json. A one-point change in recurrence changes the score by 4 points; nearby ranks are not statistically distinct. Safety, licensed scope and real supply gates override score.

The initial 18 publication categories are selected for repeat or consequential needs that can be addressed with a scoped service request and realistic manual verification. Accounting is initially restricted to reviewed registry policies for Ontario (Canada) and India; other professional jurisdictions need an explicit policy addition. An initial category is an onboarding choice, not proof that a provider is available.

## Evidence interpretation

- BLS occupation families establish that these service roles exist and describe their work. US employment is only a proxy; it cannot establish Tamil-language demand worldwide. Bookkeeping's employment outlook cautions against assuming that all repeat administrative work is growing. We therefore score concrete recurring tasks, not hype about traffic.
- [ONS language research](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/culturalidentity/language/bulletins/languageenglandandwales/census2021) supports considering language differences in service access. It does not establish a Tamil service-market gap in every country. Tamil preference and willingness-to-pay ratings remain hypotheses.
- [NCS](https://www.ncs.gov.in/) informs the separation between legitimate business services and job promises. Data-entry service listings must not become pay-to-get-work schemes.
- [CPA Ontario](https://www.cpaontario.ca/protecting-the-public/directories) distinguishes members, firms and public-accounting licences; historical entries may remain. The model therefore requires active status and permitted scope, not merely a name appearing in a directory.
- [IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/choose/authorized.html), [UK adviser guidance](https://www.gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser) and [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/choosing-a-tax-professional) show why country-specific authorization matters. These sensitive categories are not opened for general provider publication in this release.
- [NAATI](https://directory.naati.com.au/) is an official credential route for relevant Australian language services. A generic translator listing does not imply certified translation, court authorization or acceptance by a government agency.
- [ISO country codes](https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html) and [currency codes](https://www.iso.org/iso-4217-currency-codes.html) support explicit identifiers. The app does not infer currency from language and does not convert or combine prices across currencies.
- [FTC reviews guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers) informs the decision not to add fabricated ratings, testimonials or “verified” badges. This is not a worldwide legal-compliance certification.

## Initial supply strategy

Onboard a small number of owner-consented businesses in actual service areas. Prioritize bookkeeping, translation/interpreting, tutoring, computer support, websites/admin work and common household services. Do not recruit by scraping or auto-messaging providers. For each provider, verify the public identity/contact, declared language, service scope, area, listing consent and relevant credentials. Do not use “global” as a claim that every country has available suppliers.

Care, nursing, legal/immigration, finance, electrical/plumbing and other higher-risk categories remain gated until the required jurisdiction/safeguarding/clinical procedures exist. Guardians must manage tutoring contacts; no children's identifying information belongs in an enquiry.

## Validate after launch

Use voluntary task testing with Tamil-speaking families and small businesses in Tamil Nadu and at least two diaspora regions. Measure whether users correctly confirm service/city/language, reach a suitable provider, understand price/verification limits and revoke consent. Obtain real supply and actual operator-recorded outcomes before re-scoring. General page visits, service searches, drafts opened, enquiries actually received and jobs won are different quantities. Never label a click a sale. Existing Worker acceptance must be checked by the owner before claiming site-wide funnel reports. Do not export private search text or messages to research analytics.
