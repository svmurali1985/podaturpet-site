# Town and regional discovery: source register

Reviewed 26 September 2026. Baseline: the buyer/country-state update pushed as 5c08686.

Content is original English and Tamil editorial writing. Government and temple pages support factual summaries; suggested itineraries and visitor questions are editorial guidance. No external article or new photograph was copied. Search engines were used to find primary pages, not treated as the source themselves.
