# Live-site review and customer priorities

Reviewed 2 October 2026 (America/Chicago), before this package is published.

## Observed

- Homepage, enquiry page, robots.txt and sitemap.xml returned HTTP 200. Robots permits public crawling. The homepage has a canonical URL, index/follow directives and English/Tamil alternate links. Thirty-six sitemap entries existed before this change.
- Public search returned the homepage and multiple product/town pages. This establishes some discoverability; it does not prove Google indexing coverage or keyword position.
- The desktop homepage has a clear lungi-led hero, coherent colours and two visible collection/quote actions.
- A homepage test using PT-WC-01, 100 pieces and Chennai created the expected WhatsApp/email draft. The product enquiry link prefilled PT-WC-01. Tamil switched the enquiry labels and copy. No message was sent and no order was placed.
- The public counter initially displayed 66 homepage loads and 114 site-wide loads for the last thirty days. Later audit navigation increased the site total. Counts include repeat loads and may exclude untracked visitors; they are not unique buyers or sales.
- The catalogue leaves fabric, size, minimum order, packing, samples and dispatch as unconfirmed. Product detail links sometimes point to a category or the whole catalogue instead of a unique product specification.
- Buyer-facing contact is a US number and personal Gmail address. Some indexed older pages describe a sourcing platform while newer copy says the business manufactures lungis. The Oman and Chennai legacy URLs now serve HTTP 200 meta-refresh pages with canonical targets; cached search extracts still retain older text.

## Priority actions

1. Publish real verified product specifications: fabric composition, dimensions, order minimum, sample terms, packing and dispatch ranges. Add price bands only if commercially accurate. These are owner-supplied business facts; this package does not invent them.
2. State the legal/business seller identity, manufacturing or sourcing role, operating location, business hours and which team handles India enquiries. Use one consistent explanation across buyer pages. Add genuine workshop, packing and shipment evidence with permission.
3. Reduce quote friction. The draft flow works, but preparing a draft and then sending it in another app are separate actions. Track quotation handoff as a handoff, not a completed enquiry or sale. Record confirmed enquiries and orders in a simple owner-controlled log.
4. Use Google Search Console to inspect coverage, impressions, clicks, queries and country/device splits. Without this data, the exact reason for low organic traffic remains unconfirmed.
5. Distinguish owner/testing traffic, repeat loads and genuine customer sessions. Do not infer local demand from city counters alone. Check current tracking consent and coverage.
6. Replace legacy meta-refresh wrappers with hosting-supported permanent redirects when practical, and retain matching canonical targets. This is a hosting configuration task outside the local-content package.
7. Keep local discovery useful but subordinate to wholesale on the homepage. The new hub supports returning local visitors; local visitors are a different audience from wholesale buyers.
8. Obtain real-device and PageSpeed/Core Web Vitals measurements before claiming the mobile site is fast. This review did not establish LCP, INP, CLS or a full mobile accessibility result.

## Limits

Public read-only review and local content validation cannot establish revenue, abandoned enquiries, traffic-source quality or actual keyword rankings. Additional content alone does not guarantee customers or rankings. The package addresses requested discovery/navigation improvements; verified commercial information and targeted buyer acquisition remain essential.
