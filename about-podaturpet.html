Where is Podaturpet?

Podaturpet, also written as Podhaturpet, is a Town Panchayat in Pallipattu Taluk of Tiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu. The local Town Panchayat describes the town as being about 21 km from Tiruttani.

Town profile

The Town Panchayat records an area of about 8.86 square kilometres and 18 wards. Its population table reports a total population of 22,040 for 2011.

Local economy and identity

Podaturpet's local-government profile specifically mentions paddy fields along with house-to-house powerloom and handloom activity. That combination of agriculture and weaving is an important part of the town's economic identity.

Culture

The Town Panchayat highlights the Amman Festival as an important local celebration and says the event attracts people from surrounding areas.
