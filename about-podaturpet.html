Where is Podaturpet?

Podaturpet, also written as Podhaturpet, is a Town Panchayat in Pallipattu Taluk of Tiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu. The local administration places the town approximately 21 kilometres from Tiruttani and around 10 kilometres from Pallipattu. Its location connects it with the agricultural communities and textile businesses of northern Tamil Nadu.

Town profile

The Town Panchayat records an area of approximately 8.86 square kilometres, 18 electoral wards and 154 streets. Its published population table reports 22,040 residents for 2011. Because the table's separate current-population figure does not identify a clear reference year, this page uses only the dated 2011 number.

Town Panchayat history and local administration

The local town profile traces the Town Panchayat's formation to 1939 and identifies Podhaturpet as a first-grade Town Panchayat. It also notes that the present ward structure dates to 1996. Tiruvallur District Administration separately lists Podhaturpet among the district's Town Panchayats and provides the relevant Executive Officer's official contact details in its public directory.

In Tamil Nadu, a Town Panchayat is a form of local government used for places that bridge rural and urban settlement patterns. This helps explain why Podaturpet combines a recognisable town centre and public services with nearby agriculture, household-linked work and local trade.

Local economy and identity

Podaturpet's local-government profile identifies paddy fields alongside handloom activity and household-linked powerloom work. Rather than being defined by one factory or one seller, the area can be understood as a network of weaving skills, textile trade, small businesses and agricultural livelihoods.

For lungi buyers, this background provides useful context: cotton checks, traditional patterns and textile sourcing are connected to a documented regional weaving identity. Specific products, suppliers, production methods and commercial claims should still be checked individually before any order.

Handloom, powerloom and everyday textile work

Handloom and powerloom are different methods of producing woven cloth. In practical terms, both rely on the relationship between lengthwise warp threads and crosswise weft threads, while the equipment and production approach vary. Podaturpet's local profile mentions both forms of weaving, which is why the town has relevance to buyers researching different lungi styles and sourcing options.

Culture

The Town Panchayat highlights local Amman festival celebrations as part of Podaturpet's community life. These traditions sit alongside weaving, agriculture and neighbourhood commerce in shaping the town's identity. Festival dates, temple programmes and event arrangements can change, so visitors should check locally before making plans.

Nearby Tiruttani and places of interest

Tiruttani is an important nearby town referenced in Podaturpet's local-government profile. Tiruvallur District Administration identifies Arulmigu Subramaniya Swami Temple at Tiruttani as a significant religious destination. Visitors interested in combining regional travel, textile sourcing and local culture can use Tiruttani as a useful point of orientation, while confirming current transport options independently.
