Why the border matters.

The photographed lungi combines fine intersecting checks with a brighter blue band at its edge. For buyers comparing similar white designs, the border colour, border width and overall check spacing are useful details to specify early.

Recognisable blue edge

The visible blue border gives the otherwise light checked design a stronger identifying detail.

Light grid pattern

Narrow blue and grey intersecting lines create a more understated appearance across the white fabric.

Photographed reference

Use the real product photo to explain the style you want; ask about exact matching and availability.

Questions to ask about this style.

The image identifies a design, not confirmed stock, fibre composition or pricing. Clarify appearance and commercial details directly before making a purchase decision.

  • Can the blue border shade and width match the photograph?
  • What check spacing, line colour and white shade are available?
  • What are the fabric composition and finished measurements?
  • Can a sample or additional current product photos be provided?
  • What quantities, packing and destination options are possible?
  • How will price, supplier identity and delivery terms be confirmed?
