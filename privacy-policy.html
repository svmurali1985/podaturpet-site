Privacy Policy and Visitor Analytics

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Podaturpet.com provides independent textile-sourcing information, local business information, visitor guides and business-enquiry services. This notice explains what information may be processed when you visit our website and how to contact us about privacy.

Information we may collect

  • Your internet protocol (IP) address and approximate location, such as your city, region or country.
  • The page you visit, page title, referring website or page, and the date and time of the visit.
  • Interactions with website features, including product or catalogue links, advertising links, WhatsApp links, telephone links and email links.
  • Information you choose to provide when you contact us, such as your enquiry, preferred products, order quantity, delivery destination or contact details.

Approximate location is inferred from network information; the website does not ask for your device's precise GPS location. Ordinary visits do not require an account or login.

Why we use this information

We use visitor information to understand website traffic, prepare internal business and visitor reports, improve website content and services, understand interest in products and local services, respond to enquiries, protect the website, and investigate misuse or security issues. We use an appropriate legal basis for each activity and request consent where applicable law requires it.

Website analytics and service providers

Visitor events may be processed using Cloudflare infrastructure, including a Cloudflare Worker and a connected database or email service used to prepare internal reports. Service providers process information as needed to operate and protect the website and its reporting systems. Depending on the provider's infrastructure, information may be processed outside your country.

Our visitor-analytics script does not set an advertising or analytics cookie. Browser storage may be used to remember an analytics opt-out, and other website features may use browser session storage for functionality such as rotating announcements. Third-party websites that you choose to visit, including WhatsApp, operate under their own privacy policies.

Retention and access

We retain visitor and enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for reporting, security, service improvement, business communications, or applicable legal obligations. Access should be limited to authorized website operators and service providers who require the information for those purposes.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can disable future website analytics using the “Disable analytics” link in the footer. The website also respects supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals. Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of your personal information, or object to its processing.

We do not automatically receive your name, phone number or email address merely because you visit the website; those details are received only if you choose to contact us or otherwise provide them.
