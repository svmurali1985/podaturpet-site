A few useful answers before we start

What should I send in my enquiry?

Tell us your business type, the result you want, preferred language and target date. A non-sensitive example helps define the work.

What does free development mean?

The advertised website, chatbot and automation development service has no service charge. Confirm the agreed scope and any external hosting or provider arrangements before work begins.

Can you update an existing website?

Share the public website link and a list of requested changes. Access and compatibility need to be reviewed before changes are agreed. Do not send passwords in a public enquiry.

Will an enquiry send automatically?

No. The service links prepare a WhatsApp draft. Review it and choose Send yourself.
