How to request a wholesale quote

  1. Choose a design. Use the reference below its photograph.
  2. Share your requirement. Enter quantity, destination and any fabric or size preferences.
  3. Review the terms. Confirm samples, specifications, minimum order, packing, price and delivery before ordering.

Include these details in your enquiry:

  • Your preferred photographed design.
  • Approximate quantity and business type.
  • Delivery city, state or country.
  • Preferred size, colour and packing.
  • Fabric composition and sample requirements.
  • Questions about supplier credentials and terms.

Photographs show design references. Confirm availability, fabric, dimensions, price and delivery for your order.
