Modern Lungi product concepts · 2026

Traditional comfort. Modern function.

Modern Lungi is an exploratory convertible-lungi direction designed to retain woven identity while making fit, movement, storage and climate use easier for a wider global audience.

Core product direction

Modern Lungi: five modes in one adaptable garment.

The wearer chooses the mode that suits the moment, instead of needing a completely different garment.

MODE 01

Classic

Traditional full-length drape for familiar comfort.

MODE 02

City

Secure snap closure for walking and daily movement.

MODE 03

Active

Fold-and-fasten knee length with optional inner coverage.

MODE 04

Travel

Protected phone, passport and key storage.

MODE 05

Cold Climate

Optional removable lining for layered winter styling.

Functional foundation

Designed around confidence, not novelty alone.

The strongest first prototype combines a small number of features that solve real customer concerns.

FitLock waistSoft adjustment with side buckle and secure snaps.
Supported pocketsZipped storage designed to reduce pulling at the waist.
Optional inner shortsDetachable coverage for active and public use.
Length conversionHidden fasteners switch full length to knee length.

Start focused

Three prototypes to test first.

Instead of placing every feature in one product, these three focused versions can reveal what customers genuinely value.

Modern Lungi Air

A lightweight warm-weather version for home, resort and summer use.

  • Breathable woven fabric
  • FitLock waist
  • One secure pocket

Modern Lungi Travel

A secure, packable version for airports, road trips and leisure.

  • Passport and phone pockets
  • Snap closure
  • Optional inner shorts

Modern Lungi Winter

A layered concept for customers in colder markets.

  • Removable thermal layer
  • Legging-compatible fit
  • Heavier seasonal check
