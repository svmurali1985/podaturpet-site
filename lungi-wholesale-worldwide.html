Choose your order

Choose a product reference from the catalogue. Tell us whether you want mixed colours or quantities by design. If you are unsure of quantity, ask about the minimum order.

Use the sample enquiry option to ask about availability, cost and delivery. Confirm fabric composition, finished size and whether the lungi is open or stitched before approving a bulk order.

Compare the full quotation

Ask for the per-piece price, quantity, colour assortment, packing, dispatch estimate and delivery charges in writing. These details depend on the design and destination.

Plan international delivery

Share the destination country, city and postal code. Before payment, agree who arranges freight, required documents and import clearance, and which charges are included. Available arrangements must be confirmed for your order.
