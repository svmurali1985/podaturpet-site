How to wear

Four simple moves.

1. Step in and centreKeep the side pocket on your preferred side.
2. Adjust the waistSet a comfortable fit using the waist control.
3. Wrap and snapBring the front panel across and secure the closure.
4. Check movementWalk, sit and use the pocket before going out.
