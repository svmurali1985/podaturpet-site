Podaturpet Textile Story

From a line of yarn to a field of checks.

Follow the making of a woven checked lungi: yarn selection, colour planning, loom preparation, weaving and finishing.

WhatsApp Buyer Enquiry Email Enquiry

A folded checked lungi hides a series of decisions. Before cloth takes shape, someone chooses the yarn, arranges the colours and plans how the lengthwise and crosswise threads will meet. This guide explains the general process; individual suppliers use different materials, equipment and finishing methods.

1. Decide what the cloth needs to be

The starting point is the intended product: its fibre composition, size, feel and pattern. A cotton label, a soft touch and a cotton blend are not interchangeable descriptions. For buyers, the practical first step is to ask the supplier to state the composition and finished dimensions in writing.

2. Plan the checks

In a yarn-coloured woven check, colour sequences in the lengthwise warp intersect with colour sequences in the crosswise weft. Wider bands and narrow lines create different rhythms across the cloth. The same colours can look quite different when their spacing changes. Printed checks are made differently, so a photograph alone does not establish construction.

3. Prepare the warp

Many threads must be arranged alongside one another before weaving begins. Warping prepares this lengthwise yarn supply. Even tension and the intended thread order help keep the fabric consistent. The preparation route depends on the yarn and production setup.

4. Set up and weave

The loom controls groups of warp threads so the weft can pass between them. Each inserted weft is brought into place as the cloth grows. Handlooms and powerlooms differ in how motions are driven; both require decisions about yarn, construction and quality. The loom label alone is not a complete quality specification.

5. Inspect and finish

After weaving, fabric is checked and prepared for sale. Cutting, washing, edge finishing, folding and packing arrangements vary. Ask whether the supplied lungi is open or stitched, what size is measured before and after washing, and which finishing steps are included in the quotation.
