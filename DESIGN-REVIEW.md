# Podaturpet website review and redesign

11 September 2026

## Main finding

The site’s useful information was presented with too little distinction between selling products, telling the town’s story and looking up practical details. Repeated large headings, introductory paragraphs and similar cards made it feel like a reference directory. More pages alone would not solve that.

This update restructures the homepage and the principal town pages, and applies a consistent visual system to all 26 content pages. Fourteen redirect pages retain their destinations. It uses the latest website files supplied and updated in this conversation. Public page retrieval was also checked, but returned an older navigation snapshot; it was not treated as proof that the latest GitHub deployment was live.

## What changed

| Area | Problem | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Repeated collection introductions and competing messages | A clear collection → craft → town → innovation → enquiry sequence |
| Products | Actual photographs competed with styling concepts | Six real photographed designs appear together, with references and direct enquiry links |
| Craft | Weaving footage was buried inside an optional section | A dedicated video section with an explanation and link to the making guide |
| Town introduction | Facts and public contacts dominated the first impression | A photographic introduction, paths to stories and visits, then practical information |
| Stories | Long text passages had similar visual weight | Reading-focused typography, chapter spacing, a weaving image and video, and expandable reference lists |
| Public services | Long repeated card grids | More compact cards, clearer headings and expandable reference sections |
| Modern Lungi | Ideas looked like another competing homepage campaign | One clearly labelled concept section with links to innovation and partnerships |
| Typography | Multiple old overrides produced oversized headings | A consistent scale, including normal Tamil character spacing and controlled heading size |
| Engineering | Homepage presentation depended on overlapping historic patches | Removed its large inline stylesheet and three legacy presentation stylesheet imports; the new design layer owns its layout |
| Buyer form | Design shortcut buttons did not update the selected product | Buttons now select the matching reference and update the pressed state |

The existing right-side page explorer, bilingual controls, feedback, catalogue specifications, market selector and WhatsApp/email flows remain.

## Content that still limits credibility

The [catalogue](https://podaturpet.com/lungi-product-catalogue.html) needs confirmed fabric composition, finished size, minimum order, packing, samples and dispatch details. Those cannot be inferred from photographs. Keep “Ask for confirmation” until the facts are supplied.

The [town guide](https://podaturpet.com/podaturpet-town-guide.html) and [stories page](https://podaturpet.com/podaturpet-stories-and-culture.html) need original Podaturpet photographs and first-hand voices. Regional hill imagery is labelled as regional and credited; it does not claim to show the town streets.

The [directory](https://podaturpet.com/podaturpet-local-business-directory.html) still needs actual reviewed business records. Category cards are not evidence that a business is listed or verified. The contribution workflow is ready, but no fictional shops or testimonials were added.

AI Modern Lungi concepts and lifestyle images remain explicitly identified. They are inspiration, not proof of stocked products, prototypes or established export capacity.

## Next content priorities

1. Supply ten original town/workshop photographs with dates, place names and publishing permission.
2. Confirm the commercial facts for the six catalogue references.
3. Record three short interviews with weavers, with permission.
4. Add ten verified business listings and keep event dates current.
5. Review actual received enquiries separately from WhatsApp clicks, then improve the pages that attract buyers.

These steps improve authenticity and usefulness. The redesign alone does not guarantee enquiries or search rankings.

## Validation and limits

Local route, anchor, media-reference, script-syntax and content-preservation checks passed. Product shortcut behaviour and expandable-section deep links/search were checked in isolated JavaScript harnesses. No messages were sent.

This was not a live deployment, Lighthouse audit, security penetration test or browser/device visual test. The existing tracking backend was not supplied, and live analytics reporting remains unverified. See VALIDATION.md and ANALYTICS-NOTES.md for details.
