Handloom lungis and the Podaturpet weaving tradition.

Handloom is part of Podaturpet's documented identity

Podhaturpet Town Panchayat describes the town as a place with house-to-house powerloom activity and a handloom centre. That matters because the textile story here is not invented marketing language; weaving is part of the town's official local profile.

What buyers should verify

If you specifically require handloom production, ask for confirmation from the supplying business before purchase. Podaturpet.com should not treat every photographed lungi as handloom unless the production method is verified for that product.
