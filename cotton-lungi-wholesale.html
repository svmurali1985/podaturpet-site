Before asking for a wholesale quote

Prepare six details: approximate quantity, fabric preference, pattern or colour, size/specification, destination and whether you are a retailer, wholesaler or distributor. This makes it easier to discuss availability, samples, MOQ, packing and commercial terms.

Cotton lungi wholesale: order questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities are not yet published. Send your preferred design and quantity so the team can confirm the minimum for that order.

How do I get a wholesale price?

Request a quote with your design, quantity, fabric and size requirements, and delivery destination. Ask for packing, transport charges and any applicable taxes to be itemised.

Can I request a sample before a bulk order?

Ask the team to confirm sample availability, sample cost and shipping charges for your chosen design before paying. Sample terms are confirmed for each enquiry.

Are all photographed designs confirmed as cotton?

Fabric composition is not yet confirmed for every design. Request the cotton percentage, finished measurements and care instructions for the exact product you intend to order.

How are packing and delivery arranged?

Share your delivery city, postcode and country, plus any packing or labelling requirements. Confirm available delivery options, charges and the dispatch estimate in the written quotation.
